# Tallyroll store listing

**Title:** Tallyroll: Dice & Score Sheet

**Short description (80 characters max):**
Roll five dice and fill thirteen boxes in the classic Yatzy game. Free forever.

**Long description:**
Roll five dice, hold the ones you want, and roll again, up to three times a turn. Then choose a box on your score sheet: ones through sixes, three and four of a kind, full house, both straights, Yatzy and chance. Every box can be filled once, and the upper section earns a bonus at 63 points. The sheet shows what each box would score right now, so you can see your options before you commit.

Play solo and chase a high score, pass and play with up to four people on one device, or play against a computer opponent on easy, normal or hard. Daily challenge gives everyone the same dice for the same UTC date. Unfinished games are saved on your device, so you can pick them up after a reload.

Tallyroll is free, MIT-licensed, and has no ads or tracking. Your progress stays on your device.
